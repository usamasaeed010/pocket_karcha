## Long description (English)

```
Pocket Karcha is for anyone who writes expenses in a diary, on WhatsApp, or in their head — and still wonders where the money went.

Add what you spent or received in a few taps. Use You Gave and You Got the way people actually talk about money here, not confusing labels. Pick Day, Week, or Month on the home screen: Day shows only today's entries, Week covers this calendar week, Month shows the full current month.

Attach receipt photos when you need proof later. Keep a simple list of people you lend to or borrow from, with running balances. Home and History stay in sync with the same period you choose.

The app works in English and Urdu. Set PKR or another currency, rename categories, and adjust labels to match how your family phrases things.

Sign in once with email. Your transactions sync to the cloud. Profile photo and name can be updated anytime from Edit profile.

Built for Pakistan — phone numbers default to +92, sensible categories, and no clutter. Loan, SIP, and business calculators are on the app when you need a quick figure; they do not touch your ledger.

No ads in the current version. Privacy policy is available inside the app.

If something looks off after an update, pull down to refresh on Home. Questions: srndrddev@gmail.com
```
