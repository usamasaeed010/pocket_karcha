Account & Data Deletion

Pocket Karcha · پاکٹ خرچہ

Effective: May 2026

1. About This Page

This page explains how users of Pocket Karcha can delete their account and request removal of personal data. It is provided for Google Play, App Store, and user transparency requirements.

App name: Pocket Karcha
Android package: com.pocket.karcha.expense.manager
Developer contact: srndrddev@gmail.com

2. Delete Account in the App (Recommended)

You can disable your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open Pocket Karcha and sign in with your email account.
  2. Go to the More tab (مزید).
  3. Tap Settings (ترتیبات).
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm in the popup — you will be signed out immediately.
What happens: Your account is marked as disabled in our database. You will not be able to sign in again with that account. Local data on your phone (settings, people list, categories) is cleared when you sign out.

3. Request Full Data Deletion by Email

If you cannot access the app, or you want all cloud data permanently removed (profile, transactions, uploaded images), email us:

Email: srndrddev@gmail.com

Please include:

  • The email address you used to register in the app
  • Subject line: Pocket Karcha - Account Deletion Request
  • Optional: your registered phone number (helps us verify ownership)

We will verify your identity and process the request within 30 days. You may receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete.

4. What Data Is Deleted vs Kept

Data type In-app Delete Account Full deletion (email request)
Firebase Auth account (email login) Disabled — sign-in blocked Permanently deleted from Firebase Auth
Profile (name, phone, currency) Account disabled flag set Deleted from Firestore
Transactions / expenses May remain on server until full deletion Deleted from Firestore
Receipt & profile photos (Supabase) May remain until full deletion Deleted from storage
People, categories, app settings Removed from your device on sign-out Already on device only — not in cloud
Firebase Analytics (usage stats) Not linked after sign-out Anonymized per Google’s retention policy

5. Data Retention

After you use Delete Account in the app, some server data may remain for a short period until we process permanent deletion. Backup copies on Google Firebase or Supabase may take up to 30 days to be fully purged from systems.

Important: Deletion is permanent. You cannot recover your account or transaction history after full data deletion is processed.
