Pocket Karcha

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

1. Introduction

Pocket Karcha (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is a personal expense and money-tracking application designed for users in Pakistan, with support for English and Urdu. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, where it is stored, and what choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Important: The App requires a registered account (email and password) to use cloud features. Calculator tools run on your device only and do not save your inputs to our servers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & profile information

  • Email address — required to create and sign in to your account.
  • Password — stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication; we do not store your password in plain text on our servers.
  • Full name — optional at registration; may be updated during profile setup.
  • Phone number — collected during profile completion (with country/dial code, default Pakistan +92).
  • Preferred currency — e.g. PKR, for displaying amounts.
  • Language preference — English or Urdu.
  • Profile photo — if you choose to upload one (compressed JPEG).

2.2 Financial & transaction data

  • Transaction amount, date, category, note, and type (expense/income or “You Gave” / “You Got”).
  • Optional link to a person from your People list.
  • Optional receipt images attached to a transaction.

2.3 People & contacts

  • If you use the contact picker, the App reads your device contacts only with your permission so you can select someone to add.
  • We save only the contact you select: name, phone number, and country code — stored on your device, not uploaded as your full address book.
  • Credit/debit balances per person are calculated from your transactions on the device.

2.4 Images & media

  • Receipt photos — from camera or gallery, compressed before upload.
  • Profile photo — from camera or gallery, compressed before upload.

2.5 App settings (stored on your device)

  • Custom categories, terminology mode, default time period, date-range filters.
  • Session and cached profile data for faster startup.

2.6 What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers or ad tracking.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics for anonymous usage statistics (e.g. screens opened, app opens). No advertising identifiers. Your Firebase user ID may be linked to analytics events while you are signed in.
  • No access to SMS, call logs, or location (unless added in a future update).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To store and sync your transactions when you are signed in.
  • To display dashboards, history, charts, and person-wise ledgers.
  • To upload and display receipt and profile images.
  • To remember your language, currency, and app preferences.
  • To disable accounts when you request account deletion (see Section 8).

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Data type Location
Account (email/password) Google Firebase Authentication
Profile & transactions Google Cloud Firestore (users/{userId} and expenses subcollection)
Receipt & profile images Supabase Storage (bucket: pocket-karcha)
People, custom categories, settings On your device (SharedPreferences) — not synced to cloud in current version
Calculator inputs On your device only — not saved

Public image URLs: Receipt and profile images are stored in a public Supabase bucket. Anyone with the direct link may be able to view an image. Do not upload sensitive content you would not want accessible via URL.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Supabase (image file storage) — Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (required on many Android devices for Firebase) — subject to Google’s terms and policies.

These providers process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies and security practices. Data may be processed on servers outside Pakistan.

7. Data Retention & Security

  • We retain your data while your account is active.
  • Images are compressed (JPEG) before upload to reduce size; EXIF metadata is stripped from compressed images.
  • We use industry-standard providers (Firebase, Supabase) with their security measures.
  • No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Account Deletion

You can request account removal from Settings → Delete Account. When you confirm:

  • Your profile is marked as disabled in our database (isDisabled: true).
  • You are signed out and cannot sign in again with that account.
  • Your existing transactions, profile fields, and uploaded images may remain stored on Firebase and Supabase servers.

This is a soft disable, not immediate permanent erasure of all data. To request full deletion of cloud data, contact us using the details in Section 11.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access or update your profile (Manage Account in the App).
  • Change language, currency, and app settings.
  • Sign out at any time (More → Logout).
  • Revoke camera, contacts, or photo permissions in device settings.
  • Request account disable via Delete Account, or request data deletion by contacting us.
